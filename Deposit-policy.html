Deposit Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

Purpose of Deposits

Deposits protect sellers from damage, loss, theft, late returns, or failure to return an item.

Deposit Collection

Deposits may be collected at checkout when required by the seller.

Refunds

Deposits are generally refundable after the item is returned on time and in good condition.

Partial Deductions

Part of the deposit may be withheld for cleaning, repair costs, missing parts, late returns, or minor damage.

Full Deposit Claims

The full deposit may be withheld if an item is not returned, is badly damaged, is lost, or if fraud is suspected.

Damage Review

ReFresh may review photos, messages, return details, and seller reports when deposit disputes happen.

Refund Timing

Approved refunds are usually processed within a reasonable time after return review.

Disputes

Users may contact ReFresh support if they disagree with a deposit decision.
